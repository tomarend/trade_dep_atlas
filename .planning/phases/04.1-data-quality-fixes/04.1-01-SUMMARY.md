# Summary: 04.1-01 — Data Quality Fixes

## What Was Built

Fixed three data integrity bugs that degraded dashboard accuracy:

1. **Product Descriptions**: Generated comprehensive HS6 product descriptions from all BACI product_codes files (HS92–HS22). Coverage went from 30 seed entries to 6,877 entries — 100% coverage of all 4,945 HS6 codes in processed data. Later HS revisions take precedence; hand-curated entries preserved.

2. **Country Region Map**: Expanded `country_overrides.yaml` region_map from 150 to 251 ISO3 codes across 19 UN M49 sub-regions. Added 8 overrides for historical entities (DDR, CSK, SUN, SCG) and BACI aggregate codes (PUS, R20, S19, ZA1). Added 18 pycountry territory entries. All 235 BACI ISO3 codes now have region/continent assignments.

3. **Georisk Forward-Fill**: Added forward-fill logic in `pipeline/georisk.py` that extends the latest WGI year's governance scores to cover BACI years beyond WGI coverage. Year 2024 now uses real 2023 governance data instead of the 0.5 fallback. Fallback rate dropped from 100% to 14.2% (remaining are genuinely uncovered small territories).
